The Auction Flow

FROM LISTING TO WINNER

STEP 01
Admin Creates Auction
Set title, description, image, start/end time, and minimum bid. The system goes live automatically at the scheduled time.
STEP 02
Credits Assigned
Admin allocates a fixed credit balance to each registered bidder. Clean, controlled — no real money involved.
STEP 03
Bidders Compete Live
Countdown timers, live bid feeds, instant notifications. Credits lock on every bid placed.
STEP 04
Winner Declared
Highest bidder wins, credits deducted. All others receive instant refunds. Admin confirms and the lot closes.

Credit System

FAIR PLAY THROUGH LOCKING

No ghost bids. No double spends. Every bid locks credits instantly and refunds the moment you're outbid.

Credits Lock on Bid

Instant Refund if Outbid
Outbid? Locked credits return to your balance in real-time via WebSocket.
